including the original exporter author attribution and version information.
Those comments describe the tool used to generate the files; they do not
identify NVIDIA as the copyright owner of this model.

implementation, ROS, or NVIDIA libraries. References to those external tools

in `LICENSE`. Existing generation-attribution comments have been preserved.

license notices and must not be covered by the publisher's declaration by default.
